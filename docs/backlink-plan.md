# Backlink and citation plan

Backlinks and consistent business listings are the two ranking factors the
website itself cannot create. This is the list, in the order worth doing it.

**Rule one: the details below must be byte-identical everywhere.** Google matches
listings on name, address and phone. "Suite 3" in one place and "Ste 3" in
another reads as two different businesses and splits the signal.

## The NAP block — copy and paste, never retype

```
South Coast Advisory (Pty) Ltd
Suite 3, First Floor, 22 Rosslyn Road
Amanzimtoti
KwaZulu-Natal
4125
South Africa
031 903 4787

Short description (155 characters, for listings with a limit):

```
Accountants and Xero Silver Partner in Amanzimtoti. Bookkeeping, tax, payroll,
CIPC and business valuations for KZN South Coast businesses since 1980.
```

Long description (for listings that allow it):

```
South Coast Advisory is an accounting practice in Amanzimtoti, KwaZulu-Natal,
established in 1980 and led by Neil Oberholzer, a member of SAICA and a past
president of SAIPA. The practice is a Xero Silver Partner and serves
owner-managed businesses, body corporates and individuals along the KZN South

annual financial statements, tax returns and SARS representation, payroll and
EMP submissions, CIPC company secretarial work, business valuations, virtual CFO

Primary category: **Accountant**. Secondary: Tax preparation service, Bookkeeping
service, Business management consultant.

## Tier 1 — do these first (highest value, all free)

| # | Where | Why it matters | Notes |

## Tier 3 — local and relational (the ones competitors cannot copy)

These take longer and are worth the most, because nobody else on the South Coast
has them.

- **Amanzimtoti / South Coast chamber of business** — membership usually includes
  a member listing with a link.
- **Local business associations and ratepayers' groups** in Toti, Kingsburgh,
  Scottburgh and Umkomaas.
- **Sponsorships** — a school, a sports club, a golf day, a community event.
  Sponsor pages link out, and locals read them.
- **Client websites** — many clients will happily add "Accounting by South Coast
  Advisory" with a link in their footer. Ask the twenty best clients. This is
  the highest-converting link type available, and it costs nothing.
- **Suppliers and partners** — SimplePay, SmartPractice, any software or service
  SCA resells often has a partner or customer page.
- **Body corporates and HOAs** you administer — a link from the scheme's site.
- **Local press** — the South Coast Herald and similar publish business columns.
  A monthly plain-English piece on a SARS deadline is genuinely useful to their
  readers and earns a link every time.

## Tier 4 — content that earns links on its own

The library and glossary exist partly for this. Specific things worth pitching:

- The **SARS & CIPC deadline calendar** to local business groups and chambers as
  a free member resource each February.
- The **B-BBEE guide** to industry associations whose members keep asking about
  affidavits.
- The **glossary** to anyone writing about small business finance who needs a
  plain-English reference to link to.
- Offer a **guest article** to accounting-adjacent SA sites: bookkeeping software
  blogs, small business platforms, franchise networks.

## What not to do

- **Never buy links.** Paid link networks are detectable and the penalty is worse
  than the problem.
- **Never use a different phone number or address** for tracking purposes in a
  listing. It breaks NAP consistency, which costs more than the tracking is worth.
- **Do not bulk-submit** to a hundred directories through a service. A handful of
  relevant listings beats a spray of irrelevant ones, and the junk is hard to undo.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: where, date submitted, login used, status, live URL. Re-check
every six months, because directories lose data. Record it somewhere Neil can see
it too, not only Ignatius.
